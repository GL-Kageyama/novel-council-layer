# Narrative Time（Theoretical lineage of the thesis / record of sources）

The thesis **"a narrative is read in time"** is rooted in **established intellectual traditions**: narratology, reader-response theory, and hermeneutics. This document exists **only to make those intellectual sources explicit as citations**.

> **Important**: The sources listed here are intended to make the thesis's intellectual lineage explicit; **proper nouns are not used as the criteria for evaluation judgments (evaluator agent prompts / scoring)**. Evaluation is based solely on the structure of the text, not on the authority of sources (see `references/structural-calibration.md`). Sources are **kept separate from the evaluation stage** and are recorded only in this document.

## Structural description of the thesis (no proper nouns)

The thesis is described solely by the structure of the text, without relying on the authority of sources.

> **A narrative is an experience that unfolds in time within the reader's inner world.** Even the same book becomes a different book on first reading and on rereading. A book read in youth and a book read in old age are different books. To speak of a narrative's value, one must evaluate this "quality of the time of reading."

### The seven questions of narrative evaluation

| # | Question | Reason inherent to narrative |
|---|------|-----------------|
| 1 | **Quality of time** —— How does this narrative use the reader's time? | A narrative is the art of time |
| 2 | **Distribution of information** —— What is revealed to whom, and when? | The power of narrative lies in "what is revealed when" |
| 3 | **Narrative distance** —— Who narrates, and from what distance? | The novel is the art of "narration" |
| 4 | **Design of gaps** —— Are the spaces the reader must fill intentionally designed? | The novel is completed through collaboration with the reader |
| 5 | **Consonance of style and time** —— Does the style control reading speed and sensation? | Style determines the rhythm of reading |
| 6 | **Depth of rereading** —— On the second reading, does the same book become a different book? | A narrative's value accumulates at compound interest |
| 7 | **Displacement after reading** —— After finishing, does a sense that something in life has moved remain? | The narrative's end begins after reading |

## Converting questions to scoring

| Question | Lead evaluator | Scoring signal (high score = this signal is strong) |
|------|-------------|----------------------------------------------|
| **Quality of time** | reader-experience | Low interruptibility, page-turner quality, satisfaction after finishing |
| **Distribution of information** | plot-architecture | Intentionality of disclosure timing, structure of suspense / surprise / curiosity |
| **Narrative distance** | narrative-technique | Inevitability of viewpoint choice, intentional use of narrator reliability |
| **Design of gaps** | narrative-technique ＋ theme-resonance | Intentionality of blank space, presence of gaps that invite reader collaboration |
| **Consonance of style and time** | prose-style | Consonance of rhythm and content, whether reading speed is intentionally controlled |
| **Depth of rereading** | theme-resonance ＋ plot-architecture | Multi-layered meaning, new discoveries on a second reading |
| **Displacement after reading** | emotional-power ＋ theme-resonance | Persistence of the afterglow, scenes and words recalled as time passes, a sense of becoming part of one's life |

**Example of conversion**: Question 2 (distribution of information) is evaluated as the `disclosure_timing` dimension of plot-architecture. It scores how long the information asymmetry in which "only the reader knows, and the characters do not" persists, and whether it generates tension.
