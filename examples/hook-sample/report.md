## 📝 Executive Summary

> The council split on this story, and the split is the finding. Four evaluators in the 'craft' register — prose-style (82), reader-experience (82), theme-resonance (79), emotional-power (70) — judged the story a tightly controlled, atmospheric piece whose restraint, its 'sinking equals sleeping' equivalence, and its withheld ending give it real afterglow and rereading depth. Two evaluators in the 'form and psychology' register — narrative-originality (35) and character-depth (36) — judged the machine conventional (voice-calls-name, doppelgänger, three-night escalation) and the lamplighter a cipher. The remaining evaluators (plot-architecture, world-building, narrative-technique, admiration, hook, quality) landed in a competent middle band of 59–69. The aggregate lands high — current value 62, hidden potential 61 — because the story's manner of telling is genuinely strong even where its formal novelty and interiority are not.

## 🤝 Consensus

Where the council agreed: (1) the equivalence 'to sink is to sleep' is the story's load-bearing, genuinely fresh move — noted by narrative-originality, prose-style, theme-resonance, world-building, emotional-power, and reader-experience alike; (2) the prose is deliberate and restrained, controlling reading speed with short declarative beats; (3) the ending's refusal to explain why the village sinks is a deliberate disclosure choice, not an accident. Where they split: whether that withheld ending is earned restraint (theme-resonance, reader-experience, emotional-power) or a convenient open loop (plot-architecture, admiration, narrative-originality).

## 🎯 Recommended Actions

1. Treat the split as the signal: this is a work that rewards readers who value atmosphere, restraint, and thematic depth, and will under-serve readers who want formal novelty or deep character — position it accordingly.
2. Before any rewrite, decide whether the withheld 'why the village sinks' is a feature to keep or a loop to close; the evaluators split precisely there.
3. If revising, deepen the lamplighter's interiority and plant the doppelgänger through one or two details early, without touching the prose, the 'sinking = sleeping' equivalence, or the ending.
